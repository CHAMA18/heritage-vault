How it works

From scattered attic boxes to a curated, digital masterpiece — three motions carry your family's past into the future.

01 — Identification
face_retouching_natural

Intelligent identification

Our AI learns the faces of your ancestors across decades, automatically tagging loved ones in everything from grainy Polaroids to faded home videos. The longer you use it, the more it remembers — and the less you have to.

02 — Reconstruction
