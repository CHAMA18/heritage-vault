# HeritageVault Demo Voiceover

Welcome to HeritageVault — a private, living archive for the stories, people, and moments that define a family.

Most family history lives in scattered places: photo albums, letters, old recordings, and memories that are difficult to share or preserve. HeritageVault brings them together in one thoughtful, secure space.

After signing in, the Vault gives you a clear view of what your family has preserved: memories, relatives, timelines, and stories ready to revisit. It is designed to feel less like file storage and more like a home for your family’s history.

Let’s explore the Family Map. Here, you can add a relative, describe their relationship, include important life dates, and connect them to a parent or ancestor. Every connection becomes part of a living visual map, making it easier to see how generations belong together.

Next is Story Mode. This is where saved memories become meaningful narratives. You can ask questions about your archive, collect the details that matter, and shape them into stories your family can return to and build on.

Adding a memory is simple. Upload a photograph, document, recording, or video, then add the context that gives it meaning: a title, a year, and the story behind it. Your memory is securely saved to the vault and ready to share with invited family members.

HeritageVault is more than a place to store the past. It is a way to protect it, connect it, and pass it forward — so every generation has a way back to where they began.
