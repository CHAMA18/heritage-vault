## Clean Read

Heritage Atlas begins with a simple promise: never lose your family's stories. It is a private archive for the photos, letters, recordings, places, and relationships that usually live scattered across generations.

The experience starts securely. A family signs in, and their archive opens as a living workspace, not a folder of files.

Inside the Vault, the family gets an immediate picture of what has been preserved: memories, people, dates, places, and stories ready to explore.

The Family Map turns relatives into a visual constellation. Every person can be connected to a parent, ancestor, branch, memory, and moment in time.

The breakthrough is the Heritage Atlas Agent. Instead of returning a wall of text, it turns questions into answers you can see: charts, maps, timelines, and relationship diagrams.

Behind the scenes, ClickHouse powers fast archive queries across people, places, years, and source evidence. Trigger dot dev orchestrates the agent workflow so every answer is generated from the archive, not invented.

Ask where the family lived, and the answer becomes a geography view. Ask who appears most often, and the archive becomes a network. Ask how the story unfolded, and the answer becomes a timeline.

Heritage Atlas is the visual answer layer. It connects memories back to evidence, shows relationships across generations, and gives judges something explorable rather than another paragraph.

Story Mode turns the same evidence into a guided narrative. Each chapter points back to source memories, so the family can read the story and still inspect where every claim came from.

This is Heritage Atlas: an AI archive where family history becomes searchable, visual, and alive.
